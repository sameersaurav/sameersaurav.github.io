About Me

I am a PhD Research Fellow in Computational Chemistry at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur.

My research focuses on computational chemistry, specifically developing and utilizing enhanced sampling simulations to solve complex chemical and biological problems. Additionally, I maintain a strong research interest in computational catalysis and reaction mechanisms.
