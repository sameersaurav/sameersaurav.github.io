About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry at ANS College, Nabinagar, and a computational chemist who holds a Ph.D. from IIT Kanpur and a BS-MS in Chemical Sciences from IISER Kolkata.

Alongside research and teaching, I write about chemistry for general readers in English and in Hindi, for publications including Research Matters.
